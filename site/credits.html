CLEAR ABOUT THE DETAILS.

This is an independent website design demonstration, not an owner-approved official website. Its visual direction was commissioned by the repository owner. Business operating details and commercial photography permissions still need the business owner’s approval.

Photography & usage

The photographs depict The G Spot. in Lake Wales. They were found in public review listings, not supplied by the business owner. They are demo/editorial references only. No commercial reuse license or photographer permission has been verified. Credits and a preview label do not establish permission.

Before a commercial launch, replace these references with owner-supplied originals carrying photographer permission, or obtain documented licenses. Keep identifiable visitors and any third-party branding in the clearance review.

Business information

The listed business name, address and phone were cross-checked against the associated G Spot Facebook page, Roadtrippers, and Restaurant Guru. Research snapshot: September 2026. The map destination is the exact location supplied for this project.

Beer-led atmosphere, a pool table, jukebox and patio are described in public visitor accounts; they are not presented as an owner-verified current service guarantee. Call (863) 949-4243 to check before traveling.

Public opening-hour listings conflict. This site therefore says “Call for current hours.” It does not publish a menu, prices, specials, opening-status badge, weekly event schedule, payment policies, parking guarantees, access assurances, review scores or invented testimonials. Food information on an inconsistent third-party menu site was specifically excluded.

Privacy & accessibility

This static preview sets no application cookies and includes no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising scripts, embedded social feeds or map iframes. Fonts and optimized images are served with the site. GitHub Pages, as host, may process technical request data under GitHub’s privacy statement. Clicking Google Maps, Facebook or a source link takes you to a third-party service and its own policies.

Keyboard navigation, visible focus states, responsive layouts and reduced-motion preferences are supported. The gallery uses native image links without JavaScript and adds a keyboard-operable viewer when JavaScript is available. The phone and directions links work independently of scripts. Clipboard access is requested only when you press “Copy address”; no clipboard content is read.

Typography & identity

Barlow Condensed and Source Sans 3 are served as self-hosted, subsetted web fonts under their SIL Open Font Licenses. License notices are included with the deployment. The typographic wordmark is a proposed design, not a reproduction of a confirmed official logo.
