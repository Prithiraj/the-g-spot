## Experience themes, not current service guarantees

Public visitor accounts describe a biker/dive-bar atmosphere, cold beer, pool, jukebox and outdoor patio. The real reference photographs visually corroborate the frontage, blue building, wooden bar, signage/memorabilia and outdoor seating. Calls to confirm current availability are retained. No exclusive club affiliation is claimed.

| Item | Reason |

| Menu, prices, dishes, cocktails | No reliable current owner-approved offering supplied. |
| Recurring karaoke/live music times | Anecdotal mentions are not a maintained event schedule. Link Facebook instead. |
| Review rating/count | Mirrors disagree on counts and are not live Google data. Link exact Google place. |
| Cash-only, parking, pets, smoking, access policies | Customer-reported or inconsistent; require owner confirmation. |
| Owner biography, founding year, LLC age as business age | No approved narrative or actual establishment date supplied. |
| Current alcohol/food license status | Prior research was not revalidated as a current licensing assertion. |
| Formal official-site status | Repository approval does not establish business-owner authorization. Clearly marked preview. |
